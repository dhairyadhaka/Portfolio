Voice AI Agent

A standalone agent that reaches insurance payers by phone through AI voice vendors — for the payers that APIs and bots can’t reach.

Role
Product owner

Pilot
TL;DR

I ran two proof-of-concept batches with an AI voice vendor, set honest go/no-go criteria, and designed the agent as pure orchestration: it picks the right skill, translates data in and out, and interprets the result — without owning business logic.

Problem

Some answers only come from a phone call

A share of payers can only be reached by calling them. Those claims fall back to people waiting on hold — the most expensive way to check a status.

Approach

How the agent works

  1. 01Call only where neededVoice is not used for every payer: call-only payers are identified per customer first.
  2. 02Orchestration, not logicThe agent selects the skill, maps standard fields to the vendor’s format and back, and leaves business rules to the platform.
  3. 03Interpret the answerResults are sorted into Paid, Denied, Claim not on file, In process or Needs review, then checked for completeness.
  4. 04Cost-awareEvery call is expensive, so billing rules per vendor and status are part of the design, along with logging and reporting.
Results

What the pilots showed

2proof-of-concept batches across 15+ payers
97%output quality in the second batch
≥70%extraction target set as the go criterion

Extraction landed around 51% and 43% across the two batches — the dip came from a harder payer mix, not a regression. I framed 50–60% auto-resolution as the honest ceiling and positioned voice as a fallback, not a replacement.

What’s next

Full API integration with the first vendor, starting with call-only payers for selected customers, and a link back to the vendor’s audit portal so ops users can verify what was captured.

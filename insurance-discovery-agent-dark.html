Insurance Discovery Agent

An agent that finds billable insurance coverage for patients listed as self-pay or unknown coverage — callable by other agents, products and customers.

Role
Product owner
Users
Agents, internal products, customers
Sources
Two coverage-discovery providers
Status
In build
TL;DR

Rather than another integration, I specified a reusable agent: it orchestrates two discovery data providers, de-duplicates and scores what it finds, learns from feedback, and is MCP-enabled so any agent can call it.

Problem

Unknown coverage stalls claims

When a patient’s active coverage isn’t known, a claim can’t be billed correctly. Teams search by hand or write the balance off, and the same lookup is repeated across products.

Approach

Product requirements I set

  • Callable by anyone — other agents such as Claim Status and Eligibility, internal products, and customers directly.
  • More than orchestration — checks for duplicates before calling providers and keeps a feedback loop on results.
  • Confidence-aware — uses each provider’s per-patient confidence score to decide what to trust.
  • MCP-enabled — exposed the same way as every other skill on the platform.
